Step 2-B：雙軌平衡室 🕰️Step 2-B: Dual Process Room 🕰️

失落與復原雙軌擺盪模式The Dual Process Model of Coping

「理智上知道要向前看，但感性上卻不斷被回憶拉扯……」 "My head says move on, but my heart keeps being pulled back by memories…"
Source: Stroebe & Schut (1999) 雙軌擺盪模式Source: Stroebe & Schut (1999) Dual Process Model
健康的復原不是一味堅強，而是在「失落」與「復原」兩端來回擺盪： Healthy recovery is not about staying strong all the time—it oscillates between two orientations:
  • 🌧️ 失落取向 (Loss)： 允許哭泣、思念、情緒低落。 🌧️ Loss-oriented: allowing tears, longing and low moods.
  • 🌱 復原取向 (Restoration)： 逐步適應新角色、建立日常、嘗試新活動。 🌱 Restoration-oriented: adapting to new roles, building routines, trying new activities.

Step 3-A：自我關懷 🫶Step 3-A: Self-Compassion 🫶

拆箱大掃除：翻轉內在聲音Unboxing: Turning the Inner Voice Around

「是不是我做錯了甚麼？為甚麼是我？」 "Did I do something wrong? Why me?"
Source: Kristin Neff (2003) 自我關懷理論Source: Kristin Neff (2003) Self-Compassion Theory
面對關係分離與失去，大腦容易陷入自責或自憐。

心理學家克莉絲汀‧內夫提出「自我關懷」概念：
面對挫折、失敗或痛苦時，像對待好朋友般善待、接納自己，而非嚴厲批判。

👇 這三個紙箱收藏了我們可能對自己說的話。點擊拆箱，看看整理過後會變成甚麼——（再點一下可重新封箱，隨時重溫） Facing separation and loss, our mind easily slips into self-blame or self-pity.

Psychologist Kristin Neff proposed self-compassion: when we suffer, fail or feel inadequate, we treat ourselves with kindness and acceptance—like a good friend would—rather than harsh criticism.

🌍 共通人性🌍 Common Humanity
「受苦是人類的共同經驗。」"Suffering is part of the shared human experience."
遭遇困境時，我們常有一種非理性的孤獨感——彷彿「我」是世上唯一承受這種痛苦的人。但「人」的定義本身就意味著脆弱、有缺陷和不完美。當我們自我關懷，會發現苦難將我們與他人連結起來，而不是隔離。 In hardship we often feel an irrational loneliness—as if "I" were the only one bearing this pain. Yet being human itself means being vulnerable, flawed and imperfect. With self-compassion, we discover that suffering connects us with others rather than isolating us.

🧘 靜觀🧘 Mindfulness
「我留意到這份痛，但我不等於這份痛。」"I notice this pain, but I am not this pain."
以平衡、靜觀的方式面對痛苦——既不壓抑也不誇大它。放開慣常的視角，以更全面的角度看待自身處境，防止「過度認同」於痛苦的想法和感受，避免被負面情緒吞噬。 Face pain in a balanced, mindful way—neither suppressing nor exaggerating it. Step back for a broader view of your situation, preventing "over-identification" with painful thoughts and feelings, so you are not swallowed by them.

如何培養自我關懷態度？How to Cultivate Self-Compassion?

「自我關懷是一種善意的修行，而非愉悅的享受或躺平。允許自己慢慢學習，將它變成新的習慣。」 "Self-compassion is a practice of goodwill, not indulgence or lying flat. Allow yourself to learn slowly and make it a new habit."
🍵 非正式練習（隨時隨地）🍵 Informal practice (anytime, anywhere) 自我關懷休息・透過寫作探索自我關懷・支持性觸摸（例如：把一隻手輕放在心口上） Self-compassion break · Exploring self-compassion through writing · Supportive touch (e.g. placing a hand gently on your heart)
🧘 正式練習（定時進行）🧘 Formal practice (scheduled) 慈愛呼吸法——每天數分鐘，隨著呼吸把善意帶給自己。 Affectionate breathing—spend a few minutes daily, bringing kindness to yourself with each breath.
🕯️ 溫柔提示：「回燃」現象🕯️ Gentle note: "backdraft" 有些人練習自我關懷時，起初痛苦反而會加劇——敞開心扉讓愛湧入時，舊傷也隨之流出。這是正常的過程。如果感到被負面情緒淹沒，請容許自己暫時抽身： For some, self-compassion practice intensifies pain at first—when the heart opens to let love in, old hurts flow out too. This is a normal process. If you feel overwhelmed, allow yourself to step back for a while:
🌬️ 專注呼吸🌬️ Focus on breathing 🦶 感受雙腳與地面接觸🦶 Feel your feet on the ground 🍵 喝杯茶🍵 Have a cup of tea 🐈 撫摸貓咪🐈 Stroke a cat
這種照顧自己當下需要的態度，本身已經是自我關懷。🤍 Caring for what you need right now is itself self-compassion. 🤍
